# Example: Approve a Coherent Business Revision

This fictional example shows the approval boundary for Business Architect. It does not set rules for a real project.

The owner wants managers to restore archived projects. Business Architect drafts one revision saying that a manager can restore an archived project, restoration changes its state to Active, and the interface reports success or a recoverable failure. The revision includes acceptance criteria for those outcomes and can be reviewed as one coherent unit.

Whether restoration should notify every member is a separate, independent open rule. Business Architect records it in a linked draft and excludes it from the current owner review. A green light for the restore revision does not approve notifications.

If the project cannot restore an archive after a retention limit and that limit is unknown, the restore revision cannot honestly define when restoration works. It stays draft until the owner settles the dependent retention rule. Mastermind then presents the complete revised proposal for approval.
