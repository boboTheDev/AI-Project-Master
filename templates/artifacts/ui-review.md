# UI Review

This is a rendered-evidence report, not an approval of UX or design intent. Keep it under the managed project's `.project-meta/project/ui/` when material findings or a requested review need a durable record.

## Scope and baseline

Link the reviewed build or prototype, route/state scope, approved UX and design revisions, and any draft dependencies. Label an exploratory review when no approved baseline exists.

## Evidence and limits

Record browser or tool, route, viewport, state, interaction or keyboard path, and capture or test evidence. State inaccessible and unverified states. A screenshot shows one appearance, not interaction behavior.

## Findings

| Severity | Route, viewport, and state | Expected vs observed | Evidence | Owning layer | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use `blocking`, `material`, or `minor` for severity. Name UX, design system, or implementation as the owning layer. Label a subjective preference as a suggestion and an unseen state as unverified; neither is proven drift.

## Verification and open issues

Link fixes and the same route/state checks repeated after them. For an open finding, name its impact, next action, and whether a proposed UX or design revision needs owner review. Retain captures only when they help that review or future comparison.
